# Design philosophy & aesthetic

Status: evolving brief · Updated: 2026-11-16

## Intent
Build a personal world that expresses Rain's taste and ability to create. The site should feel glamorous, smooth, sophisticated, playful, and distinctive. Music, visuals, and engineering share a purpose: make something tangible that evokes a feeling. Rain wants to impress himself; employer appeal is secondary.

## Reference hierarchy
- **[Santal](https://santalarch.com/)** is the strongest anchor: aesthetics, spatial composition, typography, scroll choreography, and overall feeling.
- **[Porsche](https://shop.porsche.com/ca/en-CA)** informs precise typography, smooth interaction, thoughtful curves, and the integration of compelling product and car visuals.
- **[Wealthsimple](https://www.wealthsimple.com/en-ca)** informs color relationships, atmosphere, and exceptionally tangible, premium objects.
- **[Daniel Tan](https://danielrltan.com/)** remains an important reference for immersive personal character and confident visual identity. Its orange and bedroom scene are not selected requirements.

## Composition & typography
Use a disciplined grid, fine rules, consistent alignment, and generous empty space. Contrast large, tightly composed display typography with restrained navigation and captions. Explore uppercase selectively. Santal's inspected headings use Helvetica; their impact depends on proportion and composition. Porsche Next is a reference for engineered character, not a selected project font.

## Motion & feeling
Treat important scroll passages as scenes with a beginning, transformation, and resolution. Maintain a stable focal point while scale, depth, contrast, or framing changes around it. Explore text reveals, spatial typography, and transitions between light, dark, and image-led environments. Motion should guide attention and let the work land. Preserve readable content, direct navigation, responsiveness, reduced-motion alternatives, and good performance.

## Objects, imagery & materials
Give creations deliberate staging: an overall view, meaningful detail, and context of use. Integrate visuals into the page's atmosphere. Premium surfaces come from believable shape, beveled edges, fine texture, controlled roughness, embossed details, coherent reflections, and soft shadows. Matte faces can coexist with crisp metallic edge highlights. Excellent images or rendered assets may provide the desired fidelity; interactive 3D is an option to evaluate.

## Color
Wealthsimple's palette and its muted green and light purple treatments appeal to Rain. Explore color together with lighting, material, and background: a scene's atmosphere matters as much as individual swatches. No final palette or hex values are approved. The earlier proposed ivory / graphite / blue / ember palette was rejected and must not be treated as direction.

## Site structure
Home · Things I've Made · Sound · About · Contact. Notes / writing can link to Substack initially. Keep biography selective; allow the collection to grow with real work. Avoid inventing accomplishments or filling empty sections for appearances.

## Next decisions
Choose the Home scene's subject and interaction; compare material and color studies in context; establish typography and layout; define a small number of scroll sequences. Exact assets, palette, font licenses, motion values, graphics tools, stack, and launch scope remain open.

This brief records direction and possibilities. It does not approve every proposed effect or technology.
